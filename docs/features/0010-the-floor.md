# 0010 — The floor

**Status:** implemented 2026-11-13

## Goal

The world is no longer the company's grounds but one floor of its office building: an outer wall
with windows, a ring of corridors round a core, and every department as a room behind a thin
wall with a doorway. After this feature the figure walks an empty office floor that reads as
one, and walls are 4 px thin instead of a whole tile. The furniture is feature 0011.

## Behaviour

### Boxes

- A **box** is (x, y, width, height) in whole pixels, half-open like the figure's box of 0005:
  it covers [x, x + width) × [y, y + height).
- **In the tileset.** A tile with `solid: true` has the one box (0, 0, 16, 16). A tile without
  it has one box per rectangle object in its `objectgroup`, or none. `Tileset.solid` becomes
  `Tileset.boxes`, index: tile id, boxes in tile-local pixels. `parseTileset` rejects, with the
  file in front:
  - a collision object that is an ellipse, a point, a polygon, a polyline, a text or a tile
    object: `tile N has a collision shape that is not a rectangle`;
  - a rotation other than 0: `tile N has a rotated collision shape`;
  - a rectangle with a fractional number, a width or height below 1, or any part outside
    [0, 16) × [0, 16): `tile N has a collision shape (x, y, w, h) that is not whole pixels
    inside the tile`;
  - `solid: true` together with a rectangle: `tile N is solid and has collision shapes; only
    one of the two is allowed`.
- **In the map.** `TileMap.solids` is row-major, width × height; each entry lists the boxes of
  the cell's tiles on `ground`, `detail` and `above`, moved to **map pixels**. A cell with no
  box is open.
- **`moveBox`.** X first, then Y, as in 0005. On each axis the moved box is tested against
  every box of every cell it touches; if it overlaps any, it is moved back flush: for a
  positive displacement its far edge goes to the smallest near edge among the boxes it
  overlaps, for a negative one its near edge to the largest far edge. Half-open intervals: a
  box flush against a wall does not overlap it and slides. The displacement is assumed below
  4 px per call — the figure walks 1.333 px, a push of 0009 moves it 3 px, the snippet
  0.667 px.
- **`sightBlocked`.** The sample points of 0008, at most 1 px apart; the line is blocked when
  a point lies in a box of its cell. A 4 px wall cannot be stepped over.
- **Rule 13.** The spawn and every named point must lie inside the map and outside every box:
  `spawn (x, y) is on a solid tile (c, r)` as before, now only when the point is inside a box
  of that cell. A point in the open part of a wall cell is accepted.

### The floor

The floor is laid out for walking: corridors are six tiles wide (96 px), doorways three
(48 px), against a figure whose box is 12 px wide.

Tile coordinates are (column, row), 0-based, columns 0–107, rows 0–79; a rectangle is columns
c0–c1 × rows r0–r1, both ends inclusive. The map has the `ground` layer and the `objects`
layer and nothing else.

except the windows: on rows 0 and 79 the columns 2–105 with column mod 4 = 2 or 3; on columns 0

## Acceptance criteria

1. The game opens in the reception: the figure at (256, 718), the camera at (96, 624), carpet
   under it, thin dark walls, the opening to the corridor right of it.
2. A wall stops the figure at its strip and nowhere else: the box comes flush to the 4 px
   strip from either side, and slides along it.
3. The figure passes every doorway of the tables, and no wall where the tables name none.
4. Every room of the table except the lift and the stairwell can be entered; those two are
   closed, their doors `door-closed` tiles.
5. The meeting rooms are reached only through the side passage.
6. The outer wall is closed all round, with the windows of the rule; the camera stays within
   [0, 1408] × [0, 1100] and nothing outside the map is ever visible.
7. No seams between tiles and no shimmering edges while scrolling, at factors 3 and 6.
8. The three snippets stand where the spec says, and the lamp freezes and captures one as in
   0008; a wall strip between the figure and a snippet blocks the light. A snippet's touch
   costs overview as in 0009; a wall strip holds the push; at zero the figure wakes in the
   data protection officer's office, at (296, 504).
9. `parseTileset` reads boxes from `solid` and from collision rectangles and rejects the four
   cases with their messages; rule 13 accepts a point in the open part of a wall cell.
10. Nothing of the campus is left: no campus file, script, test or word in `src/`, `tools/`,
    `tests/`, `public/`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/spec/` or the README. Feature specs and plans of
    0001–0009 stay as written.
11. CI is green, both smoke runs.
