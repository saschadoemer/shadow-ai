# 0001 — Walk the campus

**Status:** draft
**Milestone:** M2
**Drafted:** 2026-10-31

## Goal

The player walks a figure across the company's outdoor campus on a scrolling map, is stopped by
walls, fences and water, and sees the camera follow without shimmer. This is the whole first
stage: a world you can be in.

## Scope

**In:**

- the campus map, authored in Tiled, loaded from `public/maps/campus.tmj`
- a placeholder tileset generated by `tools/gen-tiles.ts`
- the player figure with four facing directions and a walk animation
- movement, collision against solid tiles, a centred and clamped camera
- the fixed-step loop, keyboard input, integer scaling and fullscreen
- the Dockerfile, nginx configuration and CI workflow from `ops.md`, so this feature is the
  first thing that has to be green

**Out, explicitly:** NPCs, dialogue and text (M4) · the lamp, creatures and overview (M3) ·
entering buildings and map transitions (M6) · sound (after M5) · title screen and save states
(M8) · gamepad (before M5) · camera lerp or dead zone (deferred, `plan.md` §5).

## Behaviour

**The map.** 128 x 96 tiles of 16 px — 2048 x 1536 px, about 6.4 by 8.5 screens. Layers as fixed
in INV-7: `ground`, `detail`, `above`, `objects`. The `objects` layer holds one point object named
`spawn` in the courtyard. The map lays out every region of `world.md` §1 as a footprint — courtyard
with reception and the data protection officer's office, the seven department buildings, meeting
rooms, canteen, parking lot, the basement entrance to the server room — with paths, fences, green
and water as boundaries. Buildings are closed: their footprints are solid. The map's outer ring
is solid, so the player can never reach the edge.

**The figure.** 16 x 24 px sprite, drawn with its feet on the tile grid. Collision box 12 x 8 px
at the bottom centre of the sprite, so the upper body overlaps tiles behind it. Four facing
directions (down, up, left, right) with four walk frames each and one standing frame; the walk
cycle runs at 8 frames per second while moving. When moving diagonally the facing is the
horizontal component.

**Movement.** Actions `up`, `down`, `left`, `right` from arrow keys and WASD. Eight directions;
a diagonal vector is normalized so the speed is the same in every direction. Speed **80 px/s**
(*tune*). Opposite keys held together cancel. Positions are kept as floating-point numbers in
state and rounded to integers only for drawing.

**Collision.** Per step, the movement is resolved on the X axis first, then on the Y axis, each
against the solid tiles overlapping the collision box, so the figure slides along a wall instead of
sticking to it. A tile is solid when its tileset property `solid` is true (INV-7).

**Camera.** Centred on the collision box's centre, clamped so the 320 x 180 view never shows
outside the map, rounded to integer pixels before drawing. No smoothing.

**Loop and display.** 60 Hz fixed step, at most five catch-up steps (INV-3). The 320 x 180 surface
is scaled by the largest integer factor that fits the window and centred; the remainder is filled
with a dark palette colour. `F` toggles fullscreen through the Fullscreen API. On window resize the
factor is recomputed.

**Focus.** When the tab or window loses focus, all keys are treated as released, so the figure does
not keep walking.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `npm run dev` shows the campus with the figure at the spawn point in the courtyard.
2. Arrow keys and WASD move the figure in eight directions; diagonal speed equals axis speed to
   within one pixel per second.
3. The figure cannot enter any solid tile and slides along walls when moving diagonally into them.
4. The camera follows the figure and never shows anything outside the map.
5. At a window size of 1920 x 1080 the game renders at exactly 6x; at 1280 x 720 at exactly 4x;
   the rest of the window is filled with the fill colour.
6. `F` enters and leaves fullscreen.
7. Walking does not produce seams between tiles or shimmering edges at any integer scale.
8. Every PNG under `public/` passes the palette test; the tileset is 16-px aligned.
9. `tests/arch/` passes: nothing in `src/game/` imports `render/` or names the DOM.
10. The container builds, serves the game on port 8080 with the headers of `ops.md` §2, and the
    smoke test passes against it.
11. CI is green on the pull request.

## Test plan

- `engine/collision`: a corridor fixture with one solid tile; moving into it stops exactly at its
  edge; moving diagonally along it slides; a collision box smaller than a tile does not clip a
  diagonal corner.
- `engine/camera`: clamping at all four edges and in a map smaller than the view (which is
  rejected by the loader, so the test proves the rejection).
- `engine/loop`: the three timings of `testing.md` §2.
- `engine/tiled`: loads the campus fixture; rejects a map with an unknown layer, with no `spawn`,
  with a 32-px tileset.
- `game/player`: diagonal normalization, opposite keys cancel, facing follows the horizontal
  component on diagonals, animation frame advances at 8 fps.
- `game/state`: the JSON round trip (INV-1).
- Assets: palette, reserved colour unused in this stage, tileset dimensions.
- Architecture: the forbidden names and imports.
- Smoke: load, canvas present, `ArrowRight` for one second moves the player at least 60 px, CSP
  header present.

Fixtures: `tests/fixtures/corridor.tmj`, `tests/fixtures/unknown-layer.tmj`,
`tests/fixtures/no-spawn.tmj`, `tests/fixtures/off-palette.png`.

## Feel checklist

- Does the figure catch on corners when walking diagonally past them?
- Does anything shimmer or tear while scrolling?
- Does 80 px/s feel brisk but not slippery? Note the number you would prefer.
- Does the figure visibly stand "behind" a fence or wall tile below it, as intended?
- Does the campus read as a company's grounds even in placeholder tiles — can you find the
  courtyard, the parking lot, the canteen without being told?
