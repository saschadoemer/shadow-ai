# ADR-0000 — Template

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-nnnn
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced the decision: the constraint, the conflict, or the thing that did not work. Include
what was true at the time and may not stay true.

## Decision

What was decided, in the imperative. One paragraph.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and **why it lost**. This is the part nobody can reconstruct from the
code, and the part that stops a rejected option from being proposed again in six months.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it rules out. Name the accepted downsides
explicitly — an ADR with no downsides is a decision that was never really made.

## Revisit when

The concrete trigger that reopens this decision. Without one, "we might reconsider later" never
happens.

## References

Spec sections, invariants (INV-n), feature specs, milestones.
